1. OVERVIEW

This policy covers all business-related expenses incurred by Nexova employees. Expenses must be ordinary, necessary, and reasonable for business purposes. All expenses require original receipts. Employees are expected to exercise the same care with company funds as they would with their own money.

Non-compliance with this policy may result in expense rejection, personal liability for the expense, or disciplinary action.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2. EXPENSE SUBMISSION

2.1 How to Submit
All expenses must be submitted via Expensify (expensify.nexova.internal). Submit expenses within 30 days of the transaction date. Expenses older than 90 days will not be reimbursed without VP approval. Upload a photo of the original receipt for all expenses over $25.

2.2 Approval Workflow
Under $500: Manager approval only.
$500–$2,000: Manager + Finance Business Partner approval.
Over $2,000: Manager + Finance BP + VP approval.
Over $10,000: Executive approval required.

2.3 Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expense reports are reimbursed within 10 business days via direct deposit to the employee's payroll bank account.

3. TRAVEL

3.1 Booking Travel
All travel must be booked through Nexova's approved travel portal: travel.nexova.internal (powered by TripActions). Booking outside the portal requires pre-approval from Finance and will require an Out-of-Policy justification form.

3.2 Airfare
Book economy class for domestic flights under 4 hours. Business class is permitted for international flights over 8 hours or domestic flights over 6 hours with VP approval. Book at least 14 days in advance when possible. The lowest available fare within a 2-hour window of preferred departure time should be selected.

3.3 Hotels
The nightly hotel rate limit is $250 in standard markets and $350 in high-cost markets (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Singapore). Rate limits exclude taxes and fees. Loyalty points earned on company-paid travel belong to the employee.

3.4 Ground Transportation
Use Uber or Lyft for ground transportation — expense via the Nexova corporate account in the app. Car rentals are approved for trips requiring significant local travel. Standard car rental class only (midsize or below). Do not select pre-paid fuel or unnecessary insurance add-ons (Nexova's corporate card provides collision coverage).

3.5 Meals During Travel
Meal per diem while traveling: $75/day (domestic), $100/day (international). Alcohol during travel meals is reimbursable up to $20/person with manager approval. Team meals on travel should be expensed under "Team Entertainment" not individual meal per diem.

4. MEALS & ENTERTAINMENT

4.1 Team Meals (No Client)
Lunch with direct team: up to $30/person, requires manager approval.
Team dinners (celebrations, offsites): up to $75/person, requires VP approval.
All team meal receipts must list attendees and business purpose.

4.2 Client Entertainment
Client meals and entertainment: up to $150/person, requires manager approval.
Events over $500 total: require VP approval and must be logged in Salesforce as a client activity.
Alcohol at client events is reimbursable; excessive alcohol expenses may be rejected at Finance's discretion.

4.3 Prohibited Expenses
The following expenses will not be reimbursed under any circumstances:
- Personal entertainment (movies, sporting events without business purpose)
- Fines, penalties, or traffic violations
- Childcare or pet care
- Personal grooming or clothing (unless safety-required PPE)
- First-class airfare without executive approval
- Expenses for spouses or family members unless explicitly approved for a company event
- Gifts to employees over $50 (use the HR-managed recognition program instead)
- Charitable donations on behalf of Nexova (coordinate through the Nexova Foundation team)

5. CORPORATE CARD

5.1 Eligibility
Corporate credit cards (Brex) are issued to employees at the Manager level and above, and to individual contributors who travel more than 4 times per year. Apply via a helpdesk ticket — Finance approval required.

5.2 Usage Rules
The corporate card is for business expenses only. Personal purchases on the corporate card must be repaid within 5 business days. Employees are personally liable for unauthorized personal charges. The card must not be shared with other employees or used to pay for personal subscriptions.

5.3 Spending Limits
Default monthly limit: $5,000. Travel-intensive roles: $15,000. Limit increases require Finance approval and are reviewed quarterly.

6. HOME OFFICE & REMOTE WORK EXPENSES

New remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $1,000 to purchase desk, chair, monitor, or other equipment. Stipend must be used within 90 days of start date. Submit receipts via Expensify under "Remote Setup." Annual refresh stipend of $300 is available after 12 months for supplies and equipment maintenance.
