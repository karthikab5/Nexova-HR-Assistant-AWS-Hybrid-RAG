3. BENEFITS ENROLLMENT

3.1 Enrollment Deadline
You have 30 days from your start date to enroll in benefits. If you miss this window, you must wait until the next open enrollment period (November) unless you have a qualifying life event.

3.2 Health Insurance
Log in to workday.nexova.internal → Benefits → Enroll. Choose from Basic, Plus, or Family tiers. Coverage begins on your first day of employment. Review plan comparisons at portal.nexova.internal/benefits.

3.3 401(k) Enrollment
Nexova's 401(k) is administered by Fidelity. Set up contributions at netbenefits.fidelity.com (Nexova plan code: NXV2024). Nexova matches up to 4% of your base salary. Recommended: contribute at least 4% on Day 1 to maximize the match from your first paycheck.

3.4 Other Benefits
- Mental health: $100/month Wellbeing stipend (gym, therapy, meditation apps)
- Learning & development: $2,000/year education stipend for courses, books, conferences
- Commuter benefits: Pre-tax commuter account via WageWorks (office employees)
- Employee stock purchase plan (ESPP): Enroll during first open enrollment
- Life and disability insurance: Automatically enrolled, effective Day 1

4. YOUR FIRST 30 DAYS

Week 1: Orientation & Setup
- Complete all Day 1 checklist items
- Finish mandatory compliance training (Security Awareness, Code of Conduct, Privacy)
- Meet every member of your immediate team (1:1 meetings)
- Shadow at least one customer call or demo with your manager
- Review your team's Confluence space and recent sprint notes

Week 2: Learning & Context
- Get access to and review the last 3 quarterly business reviews (QBRs)
- Complete your role-specific onboarding track in Workday Learning
- Attend all team stand-ups and sprint ceremonies
- Set your 30/60/90 day goals with your manager in Lattice

Week 3–4: Contributing
- Take ownership of a starter task or project defined by your manager
- Present a brief "what I've learned" to your team
- Complete 360 intro survey in Lattice
- Schedule 1:1 with your skip-level manager

End of Month 1: Checkpoint
Your manager will schedule a 30-day check-in to discuss your experience, answer questions, and confirm your 60/90-day plan. This is a two-way conversation — share honest feedback.
