1. EMPLOYMENT POLICIES

1.1 Equal Opportunity Employment
Nexova Technologies is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, sexual orientation, or gender identity. All hiring, promotion, and termination decisions are based solely on job-related qualifications and performance.

1.2 At-Will Employment
All employment at Nexova Technologies is at-will unless a written contract specifies otherwise. Either the employee or Nexova may terminate the employment relationship at any time, for any reason, with or without notice.

1.3 Background Checks
All new hires must pass a background check prior to their start date. This includes criminal history, employment verification, and education verification. Offers of employment are contingent on satisfactory background check results.

2. WORKING HOURS & ATTENDANCE

2.1 Standard Working Hours
Standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time. Employees are entitled to a 1-hour unpaid lunch break. Flexible working arrangements may be approved by the employee's direct manager and HR Business Partner.

2.2 Remote Work Policy
Employees in roles designated as "Remote-Eligible" may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and a signed Remote Work Agreement. Employees must be available during core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their team's primary time zone.

2.3 Attendance & Punctuality
Employees are expected to maintain reliable attendance. More than 3 unexcused absences in a 90-day period may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Employees must notify their manager at least 30 minutes before their shift start if they will be absent or late.

2.4 Overtime
Non-exempt employees will be compensated at 1.5x their regular rate for hours worked beyond 40 per week. Overtime must be pre-approved in writing by the employee's manager. Unauthorized overtime may result in disciplinary action.

3. LEAVE POLICIES

3.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
Nexova provides a flexible PTO policy. All full-time employees receive unlimited PTO, subject to manager approval. Employees are expected to take a minimum of 10 days off per year. PTO requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance for planned absences exceeding 3 days.

3.2 Sick Leave
Employees may use up to 10 sick days per calendar year without deduction from PTO. Sick days are not carried over to the following year. A doctor's note may be required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive sick days.

3.3 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave may begin up to 4 weeks before the expected birth or adoption date. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or placement.

3.4 Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). Up to 3 paid days are provided for extended family (grandparents, in-laws, aunts, uncles). Additional unpaid leave may be granted with manager approval.

3.5 Jury Duty
Nexova provides paid leave for employees summoned for jury duty. Employees must provide the jury summons to HR within 3 business days of receipt. Employees are expected to return to work on any day they are dismissed early from jury service.

4. COMPENSATION & BENEFITS

4.1 Pay Schedule
Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays. If a payday falls on a holiday, payment will be made on the preceding business day. Paystubs are available via the Nexova Employee Portal (portal.nexova.internal).

4.2 Performance Reviews & Salary Adjustments
Annual performance reviews are conducted in January. Merit increases, if applicable, take effect on April 1st of each year. Off-cycle salary adjustments require approval from the employee's VP, HR Business Partner, and Total Rewards team.

4.3 Health Benefits
Nexova offers three health insurance tiers: Basic (employee only), Plus (employee + one dependent), and Family. Open enrollment occurs each November for the following calendar year. New hires may enroll within 30 days of their start date.

4.4 401(k) Retirement Plan
Nexova matches employee 401(k) contributions up to 4% of base salary. Employees are eligible to enroll after 90 days of employment. Vesting schedule: 25% after year 1, 50% after year 2, 75% after year 3, 100% after year 4.

4.5 Stock Options
Full-time employees in bands IC4 and above receive stock option grants. Options vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. New grants are issued annually at the February Board meeting, subject to board approval.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT

5.1 Professional Behavior
All employees must treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect and professionalism. Harassment, bullying, or discriminatory behavior of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in immediate termination.

5.2 Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to HR and their manager. This includes outside employment, board memberships, financial interests in competitors, and personal relationships with vendors or customers.

5.3 Confidentiality
Employees must protect Nexova's confidential information including trade secrets, financial data, customer lists, and product roadmaps. This obligation continues for 2 years following separation from Nexova.

5.4 Social Media
Employees must not disclose confidential company information on social media. Employees speaking publicly about Nexova in a professional capacity should clarify they are sharing personal views. Nexova's communications team must pre-approve any public statements made on behalf of the company.

6. DISCIPLINARY PROCESS

6.1 Progressive Discipline
Nexova follows a progressive discipline process: (1) Verbal Warning, (2) Written Warning, (3) Performance Improvement Plan (PIP), (4) Termination. The company reserves the right to skip steps for serious violations including theft, harassment, or violations of the data security policy.

6.2 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
A PIP will specify measurable goals and a timeline (typically 30–90 days). HR and the employee's manager will conduct bi-weekly check-ins during the PIP period. Successful completion of a PIP does not guarantee continued employment.

6.3 Grievance Process
Employees who believe they have been treated unfairly may file a written grievance with HR within 30 days of the incident. HR will acknowledge the grievance within 5 business days and complete an investigation within 30 days.

7. SEPARATION

7.1 Voluntary Resignation
Employees are requested to provide a minimum of 2 weeks' written notice. Manager-level and above employees are expected to provide 4 weeks' notice. HR will conduct an exit interview within the final week of employment.

7.2 Involuntary Termination
Employees terminated without cause will receive severance based on tenure: less than 1 year: 2 weeks; 1–3 years: 4 weeks; 3+ years: 1 week per year of service up to 26 weeks. Severance is contingent on signing a separation agreement.

7.3 Return of Company Property
All company equipment, access cards, and materials must be returned on or before the final day of employment. Failure to return equipment will result in deductions from the final paycheck to the extent permitted by law.
